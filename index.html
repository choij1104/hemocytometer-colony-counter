2 · Count cells on the hemocytometer

Improved Neubauer ruling, 3 × 3 mm, 0.1 mm deep. Pick a square on the grid. On the enlarged square, tap where each cell sits; tap a mark to remove it. In Center 5 mode each 0.2 mm square opens on its own; step through 1–5.

Large square: 1 mm × 1 mm × 0.1 mm = 10⁻⁴ mL. Center medium square: 0.2 × 0.2 × 0.1 mm = 4 × 10⁻⁶ mL; the five used for small cells (80 small squares) total 2 × 10⁻⁵ mL. N = ΣC ÷ [(1 × n₁) + (0.1 × n₂)] × d, using plates inside the range from two consecutive decimal dilutions; d is the first dilution counted. For other step sizes the same weighting is applied: ΣC ÷ (n₁·D₁ + n₂·D₂). Divided by the volume plated. Report two significant figures; a third digit of 5 rounds up only when the second digit is odd.
